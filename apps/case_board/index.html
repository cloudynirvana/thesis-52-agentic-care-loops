A diagnosis is not a plan

Each case board turns one pathology report into a closed loop of care: every action has an owner, every result is recorded as an observation, and every observation opens the next step or changes the pathway. Evidence, matched trials and a drug-delivery model update with it.

Observe → Decide → Act → Update ↺

Research only. These boards organise published evidence for tumour-board discussion and expert review. They are not medical devices and do not stage, prescribe or dose. Every case on this site is synthetic. No patient data is published here.

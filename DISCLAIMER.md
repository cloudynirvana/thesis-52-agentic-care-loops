# Disclaimer

## What this deposit is

Thesis #52 is a **computational and health-systems research manuscript at protocol stage**,
together with a static demonstration page running an invented case. It is:

- **NOT** a medical device
- **NOT** clinical decision support in use
- **NOT** a diagnostic, prognostic, staging or therapeutic tool
- **NOT** approved or cleared by the FDA, EMA, NAFDAC or any other regulatory body
- **NOT** a validated system, and **NOT** a claim of clinical benefit

## No results are reported

The framework here has **not been evaluated**. There is no concordance figure, no measured time
saving, no detected-error rate and no patient outcome. The four studies in Chapter Three are
**proposed designs with stated hypotheses**, and none has been run. Any number in this
repository is either a figure taken from a cited publication, or a value invented for the
synthetic demonstration.

## The demonstration case is fabricated

The case in `apps/case_board/demo.html` is invented. Its report text, measurements, biomarker
results and findings do not come from any patient. Four misreadings are seeded in it on purpose,
as test items chosen by the author. They are **not** findings about an agent's performance.

## Nothing here is medical advice

The pathways shown are organised from published guidelines and randomised trials for
**discussion at a tumour board and for expert critique**. They do not stage a patient, do not
prescribe, and do not dose. Treatment decisions belong to the treating clinicians, who hold the
patient's full clinical picture, local resources and consent.

Effect sizes and trial results quoted in this repository must be checked against their primary
sources before clinical use. Verification status is recorded per claim in
`docs/claims_ledger.csv`, and unverified statements are marked `[CITATION NEEDED]` in the
manuscript.

## Patient privacy

No real patient report, image, identifier, date of service, hospital name or record number is
committed to this repository, in any branch. See `docs/PRIVACY_AND_CONSENT.md` for the rules and
for what is required before any real case is used.

Any translation of this framework toward clinical use must pass through:

3. Prospective evaluation with pre-registered safety metrics
